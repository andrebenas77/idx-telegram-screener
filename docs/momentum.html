Reading the price columns. All prices are the last completed session — this runs before the open, so there is no “today” yet. Trend is HH/HL when the last session made both a higher high and a higher low, LH/LL when it made neither, mixed otherwise; ▲ marks a close above the prior 5-session high. CLV is where the close sat in the session’s range — 100% is a close on the high, 0% sold into the low. CMF is 20-session Chaikin Money Flow: positive means volume transacted on strength, negative on weakness.
These three are read-outs, not filters — nothing is excluded from the board because of them. Note CMF infers from where each close sat in its range; it is not a true volume-at-price profile, which would need intraday data unavailable before the open. A session that opened weak, rallied and closed strong scores as accumulation even if most volume changed hands near the low.
