How to read this

1. The atom. For every 5-minute bar, clv = (close − low) / (high − low) — where in its own range the bar closed. Rescaled to 2·clv − 1 it runs −1 (closed on the low) to +1 (closed on the high). The session figure is the average of those, weighted by each bar’s volume divided by what that time of day normally does for that stock. IDX volume is U-shaped; without that normalisation the open and the close would decide every reading.

A day can rise hard on mild flow (it gapped up, then each bar closed near its own low) or fall on strong flow (price pinned, volume printing in the upper half of bars). That second case is the divergence this board exists to look for.

4. From one session to the sorter. sflow5 is the mean of those five = +0.1498. rel subtracts this stock’s own median sflow5 over the prior 60 sessions (-0.0013), giving +0.1512 — above this session’s top-quintile cut of +0.1067. rel is the only number that decides membership. It asks whether the tape is unusual for this name, not whether it is high against other names, which would only rank stocks by their permanent character.

5. What these numbers do not say.

  • Never read one session. Split a session’s bars into odds and evens and the two halves agree only 0.270. A single sflow is mostly noise; only the 5-session mean carries much (implied 0.649), which is why the board sorts on it.
  • The edge over “did it go up” is unproven. The instrument beats the best price-only rival by +0.251, but the date-clustered 10th percentile is -0.002 on an effective 20 tape-days. Probably real; not established.
  • There is no “rising” reading. The daily level has lag-1 autocorrelation +0.065 — white noise. A rel that grew since last week is a difference-filter artefact, not a build.

6. This is not evidence of accumulation. Trend (HH/HL), RSI, CMF, CLV and sflow are all computed from price bars. Higher-high/higher-low says the price went up. RSI says the price went up. CMF says it closed in the upper half of daily ranges; sflow says the upper half of 5-minute ranges. Four views of one fact at four resolutions — stacking them feels like corroboration and is largely the same evidence counted again. overlay_test.structure() says as much about CMF in its own docstring: it infers from where the close sits, and is not a volume-at-price profile.

For actual accumulation evidence, two things already exist and neither is on this page: the momentum board’s Net accum / %ADTV / Brokers accumulating columns, which are real rupiah from real broker codes and are independent of price by construction; and /check in the bot, which is the vendor’s true aggressor flag — definitive, and 65–300 requests per stock-day, which is why it is not a board. This board sits between them: cheaper than the tape, finer than the daily bar, and unproven.

7. So use rel as a reason to look, never a reason to size. CONFIRM is the only cell with inherited support, because it sits inside the validated momentum gate.
